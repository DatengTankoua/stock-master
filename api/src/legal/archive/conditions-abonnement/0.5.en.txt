Subscription Terms
Version 0.5, updated on 13 October 2026.
These terms describe the free trial and the paid subscription to Stock Master: what they include, how long they last, their price and what happens when they end. They supplement the Terms of Use.
1. What the subscription includes
There is a single offer, with all the functions of Stock Master. The subscription is taken out for a shop, not for a person:
- all members of the shop are included, with no extra charge per seller;
- each member accesses the functions according to the rights the shop has given them;
- the subscription is managed by the shop owner, in Organisation, then Subscription.
Included capacity. Each organisation can keep up to 1,000 product records, including those placed in the trash; each catalogue product is one record. Stock quantities do not count towards this limit, nor do sales and their history. This capacity is the same for every length. Once it is reached, only the creation of new records is refused.
2. Free trial
- A free trial of 7 days starts automatically when the shop is created.
- No means of payment is requested for the trial.
- A shop is entitled to only one trial.
- If the “End of trial or subscription” notifications are turned on, a reminder is sent within the 24 hours before the end.
- At the end of the trial, access to the shop is limited as described in the “Expiry” section.
3. Lengths and prices

The applicable price is the one in force when the payment is requested; it is shown to you before any payment. A payment already requested and a period already granted are never changed by a price change.
4. Start and length of a period
- A period is counted in calendar months: a one-month period started on 10 March ends on 10 April at the same time. If the day does not exist (31st), it ends on the last day of the month.
- A period granted before the end of the trial or of the current period starts at the end of that period: time already granted is never lost.
- The subscription status and its dates are shown in Organisation, then Subscription.
5. Payment and renewal
The owner pays from Organisation, then Subscription:
- they choose a length; the amount is set by Stock Master at that moment;
- they pay this amount once on the secure page of SasPay, the payment provider, by Mobile Money (MTN or Orange);
- the period is granted once Stock Master has obtained confirmation of the payment from SasPay; simply returning to Stock Master is not enough. The “Check payment” button runs this check again;
- there is no automatic renewal or automatic payment: each new period is the owner's decision.
If you have any difficulty, contact support@stock-master.app.
6. Expiry
At the end of the trial or of the last paid period, without a new period:
- access to the shop's pages is blocked for all its members, including on devices already logged in;
- the owner sees a screen inviting them to renew; the other members are asked to contact the owner;
- sales recorded without a connection and not yet sent stay on the device; they are sent when access is restored, within 14 days of being entered;
- the shop's data is not deleted automatically on expiry.
7. Stopping, withdrawal and refunds
As nothing is renewed automatically, simply not renewing is enough to stop the subscription at the end of the current period.
A paid period is not refunded in the event of a change of mind, non-use of the service or voluntary stopping, including when the payment was made before the period starts.
- As an exception, when an error attributable to Stock Master causes harm to the customer, the customer may obtain a refund, after the incident has been checked.
- A confirmed double charge for the same purchase is not owed: the amount charged twice is refunded.
To report an incident or a double charge, write to support@stock-master.app, stating the shop name and the payment reference.
These rules apply without prejudice to the rights that the applicable law grants you on a mandatory basis, including any right of withdrawal.
8. Who accepts these terms
These terms bind the person who creates the shop and becomes its owner. They accept them by ticking the box provided at sign-up, together with the Terms of Use. This box is never ticked in advance.
Invited administrators and sellers accept the Terms of Use, not these terms. After a transfer of ownership, the new owner's agreement will be requested in the application.
The proof of acceptance is recorded by the server as described in the Terms of Use, together with the shop concerned.
9. Complaints
For any question or complaint about the trial, a period or a payment, write to support@stock-master.app stating the name of the shop and the owner's email address.
